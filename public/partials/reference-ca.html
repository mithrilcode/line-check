If a cooling check fails

Missed 70°F in 2 hours: reheat to 165°F and restart cooling, or discard. Missed 41°F at 6 hours: discard.

Exclude or restrict staff

  • Exclude anyone with vomiting or diarrhea until 24 hours symptom-free
  • Exclude for jaundice, or a diagnosis of norovirus, hepatitis A, Shigella, Shiga toxin E. coli, Salmonella Typhi or non-typhoidal Salmonella
  • Restrict sore throat with fever, and cover infected cuts or burns on hands
  • Call your county environmental health office when two or more staff have the same diagnosed illness

What can close you

An imminent health hazard lets the inspector suspend your permit on the spot. Stop operating and call your county yourself if you have:

  • No hot water, or no potable water
  • Sewage backing up
  • A vermin infestation
  • A power outage long enough to lose refrigeration
  • Fire or flood damage

Agencies

  • Health permit & inspections: your county environmental health department (Berkeley, Long Beach, Pasadena and Vernon run their own). CCDEH directory
  • Liquor license: Dept. of Alcoholic Beverage Control
  • Seller's permit & sales tax: CDTFA
  • Workplace safety plans: Cal/OSHA
  • Hood, suppression, extinguishers: your city or county fire marshal
